Sonko Holidays – Privacy Policy and Booking Terms

Last updated: 24 October 2026

1. Who we are

Sonko Holidays operates more than ten guest houses in The Gambia, in Brikama and in other towns, with more locations planned. Some properties are currently being refurbished and will be added to the app as they reopen. We also provide the Sonko Holidays mobile app. In this policy, "we" and "us" means Sonko Holidays, and the person responsible for your data is Omar Sonko. You can contact us at customer.service@sonkoholidays.com.

2. What we collect

When you make a booking in the app we collect:

  • your name, phone number and email address;
  • your stay details: the property, check-in and check-out dates, and the number of adults and children;
  • your booking reference, payment method chosen (Wave or card) and payment status.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising and no analytics tracking.

2a. Payment details

Wave: you pay from your own Wave app. We see only the booking reference and whether the owner has confirmed the payment, not your Wave PIN or account details.

Card payments: when card payment is available, it is handled by our payment provider, Stripe. Your card details are entered on Stripe's secure page and are never stored by us or visible to us.

3. How we use your data

  • to reserve the dates you request and prevent double bookings;
  • to confirm your payment and keep your booking record;
  • to contact you about your stay, by phone, WhatsApp or email;
  • to notify the owners of new bookings by email.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase – stores booking records in a secure database;
  • Resend – sends email notifications;
  • Stripe – processes card payments (when enabled).

Only the property owners and authorised staff can see booking records. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep booking records for as long as needed to run our business and meet legal and accounting requirements, which we currently set at 6 years, and then delete or anonymise them.

6. Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you. To do so, email us at customer.service@sonkoholidays.com. We will reply within a reasonable time. We may need to keep some details where the law requires it.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete the personal data we hold about you at any time. To do so:

  1. Email customer.service@sonkoholidays.com with the subject "Delete my data" (or contact us by WhatsApp or phone using the buttons in the app).
  2. Tell us your name, the phone number or email you booked with, and your booking reference if you have it, so we can find your records.
  3. We will confirm your identity if needed and then carry out the deletion.

What we delete: your name, phone number and email address, and the details of your stay.

What we may keep: a minimal record of the booking (booking reference, dates, amount and payment status) with your personal details removed where possible, because we need it for accounting and legal purposes. We keep such records for up to 6 years and then delete them.

7. Security

Bookings are stored in a protected database with restricted access, and the app communicates with it over an encrypted connection. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

8. Children

The app is intended for adults making travel bookings. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Children are recorded only as a guest count.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the date at the top when we do.

Booking terms

  • Holding a booking: after you book, your dates are held for 30 minutes. If we do not receive payment in that time, the booking is cancelled automatically and the dates become available again.
  • Prices: prices are set in pounds sterling. For Wave payments the amount is converted to dalasi at the rate shown on the payment screen, currently D98 per £1.
  • Wave fee: Wave payments include a fee paid by the guest, shown on the payment screen (currently D10 for every D1,000).
  • Confirmation: your booking is confirmed once the owner has received your payment and marked it as paid. You will see "Payment received" in the app.
  • Cancellations and refunds: All payments are non-refundable unless you cancel at least 24 hours before your check-in time. If you cancel at least 24 hours before check-in, you will receive a refund. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before check-in, and no-shows, are not refunded. To cancel a paid booking, contact us at customer.service@sonkoholidays.com or by WhatsApp or phone using the contact buttons in the app. You can also cancel a booking that is still awaiting payment from the payment screen in the app.
  • Guests: each property has a maximum number of guests, which cannot be exceeded.
